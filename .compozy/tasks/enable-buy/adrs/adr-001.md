# ADR-001: Post-Event Checkout Conversion

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-06-29

## Context

The original ORGANIZE-$E landing page switched to waitlist mode after the live workshop date. That behavior matched the first launch plan, but visitors are now asking to buy the workshop recording after the live event has passed.

The business problem has changed from "capture late interest for a future edition" to "make it clear that the recorded workshop is available for purchase now." The page already has an established paid offer, a Kiwify checkout URL, price anchoring at R$ 47, and guarantee messaging. The highest-value MVP is therefore to remove the post-event purchase blocker and clarify the recording offer.

## Decision

The post-event page state will prioritize checkout conversion instead of waitlist capture. The page will replace the waitlist form with a Kiwify checkout button, position the offer as immediate access to the workshop recording for a limited time, keep the R$ 47 price, and reinforce the existing guarantee near the purchase action.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Replay Offer Refresh

- **Description**: Reposition the entire page as a recorded-workshop sales page, including the hero, deliverables, FAQ, objection handling, and final CTA.
- **Pros**: Creates stronger message consistency across the full page.
- **Cons**: Requires broader copy changes and increases review scope.
- **Why rejected**: The current need is to stop losing motivated buyers and reduce confusion quickly, not to rebuild the full sales narrative.

### Alternative 2: Hybrid Buy + Waitlist

- **Description**: Show the checkout CTA as the primary action while also preserving a secondary waitlist path for a future cohort.
- **Pros**: Captures future-edition interest while allowing immediate purchase.
- **Cons**: Adds competing calls to action and can distract visitors who are ready to buy the recording.
- **Why rejected**: The MVP success criterion is reducing inbound questions by making the recording purchase path obvious.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Late visitors can immediately buy access to the recording instead of finding a dead-end waitlist form.
- The page answers the most common post-event question directly: the recording is available.
- The change preserves the existing checkout, price, guarantee, and sales-page structure.

### Negative

- The page will collect fewer waitlist leads for future editions while the recording offer is active.
- Some copy outside the primary CTA areas may still mention the live workshop unless included in a later refresh.

### Risks

- Visitors may expect live interaction if surrounding copy is not adjusted enough. Mitigation: update the primary CTA and nearby copy to clearly say "recording" and "access."
- "Limited time" urgency can feel vague if no deadline is visible. Mitigation: use restrained urgency copy and avoid unsupported scarcity claims.

## Implementation Notes

The PRD should stay business-focused. It should define the required user-facing behavior: post-event visitors see a purchase CTA for the recording, not a waitlist form. Technical details belong in the TechSpec.
